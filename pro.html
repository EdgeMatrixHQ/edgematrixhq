FAQ

What is TradingView?

TradingView is the world's largest social trading platform. It has live charts for all major markets — stocks, futures, forex, crypto, commodities. Most serious traders use it. EdgeMatrix Pro runs as an indicator inside TradingView, right on your chart where you need it.

Do I need TradingView Premium?

Yes. You need TradingView Premium to add custom indicators from other authors (like EdgeMatrix Pro). A Premium subscription costs $14.95/month or $149/year. This is separate from EdgeMatrix Pro pricing.

What markets does EdgeMatrix Pro support?

Futures (micro and standard contracts), Forex, CFDs, and spot commodities like gold (XAUUSD). If TradingView supports it, EdgeMatrix Pro works on it. See the full list above.
