Getting Set Up

Three steps to live.

Follow the video, or use these steps as your reference.

01

Send your TradingView username

Reply to your confirmation email with your TradingView username (not your email). We'll send your private indicator invite within 24 hours.

02

Accept the invite on TradingView

You'll receive a notification on TradingView. Open Settings → Invite-Only Scripts and accept the EdgeMatrix Pro invite. The indicator now lives in your library.

03

Add to chart, customize, and trade

Open any chart, click Indicators, find EdgeMatrix Pro under Invite-Only. Set your confluences in the indicator settings. You're live.
